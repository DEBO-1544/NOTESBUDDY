# Notes Buddy

Notes Buddy is a collaborative note-sharing platform built for students. It provides a centralized place where students can upload, discover, and access academic notes relevant to their course, stream, semester, and subjects.

The goal of Notes Buddy is to reduce the hassle of searching through multiple WhatsApp groups, Google Drives, and social media channels to find study materials. Students can contribute their own notes, help others learn, and build a trusted academic resource for their college community.

## Features

* Secure authentication using Clerk
* Student onboarding with college, stream, and year information
* Upload and share academic notes
* Browse notes by subject, stream, and semester
* Bookmark notes for future revision
* Contributor levels and recognition system
* Search and discover relevant study materials
* Scalable architecture for future AI-powered search and recommendations

## Tech Stack

* Next.js
* TypeScript
* Tailwind CSS
* Clerk Authentication
* Zustand
* Prisma ORM
* PostgreSQL
* Cloudflare R2 (File Storage)

## Vision

The long-term vision of Notes Buddy is to become a student-first academic knowledge platform where high-quality notes are easily accessible, searchable, and organized, helping students learn more efficiently and collaborate with their peers.
